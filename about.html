Why I chose BSIT

I chose Bachelor of Science in Information Technology because I have always been curious about technology and how things work behind the screen. I've become interested in understanding how websites, applications, and computer systems are created.

At first, I didn't know much about programming or how everything worked. But as I started learning, I realized that there's so much more to discover. Every line of code, every error, and every project gives me an opportunity to learn something new.

I know that the journey won't always be easy, especially as a beginner, but I'm willing to keep learning, exploring, and improving my skills. I chose this path not because I already know everything, but because I want to discover what I'm capable of creating.
